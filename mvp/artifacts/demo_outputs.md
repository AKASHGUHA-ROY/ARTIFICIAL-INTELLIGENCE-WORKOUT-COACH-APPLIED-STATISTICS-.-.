## Demo Case 1: Busy student, beginner leg workout

**Input:**
- Target muscle group: legs
- Available equipment: no equipment
- Workout duration: 19 minutes
- Experience level: beginner
- Current effort level: 3
- Pain status: no
- Extra notes: I am an extremely busy student and live in a crammed dorm room . .

**Output:**

**Recommended Next Step:**  
Do a 19-minute legs session using bodyweight squats to a chair, glute bridges, calf raises, and light mobility . 

**Reason:**  
Use a compact workout with 2 to 3 main exercises. Use beginner-friendly movements and moderate intensity. Use bodyweight, wall-based, or floor-based movements.

**Safety Adjustment:**  
Move with control and stop if pain appears .

**Alternative Easier Option:**  
Use fewer reps, shorter sets, or an easier bodyweight variation.

**Progression / Replan Note:**  
Effort is manageable, so continue with controlled progression.

  ## Demo Case 2: Pain-aware leg workout response

**Input:**
- Target muscle group: legs
- Available equipment: no equipment
- Workout duration: 30 minutes
- Experience level: beginner
- Current effort level: 7
- Pain status: yes
- Extra notes: Having ankle pain after performing lunges and pain in the hips while performing leg raises.

**Output:**

**Recommended Next Step:**  
Stop the painful movement for now and switch to a pain-free option.

**Reason:**  
Pain is a safety signal, so the assistant should not recommend pushing through it.

**Safety Adjustment:**  
Avoid sharp, worsening, or unstable pain. If pain continues, stop the session and consider getting help from a qualified professional.

**Alternative Easier Option:**  
Do light mobility, walking, or an upper-body/core movement that does not trigger pain.

**Progression / Replan Note:**  
Return to normal training only after the movement feels pain-free and controlled.
