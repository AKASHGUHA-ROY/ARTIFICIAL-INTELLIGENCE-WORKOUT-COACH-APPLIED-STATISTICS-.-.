# Improved Outputs

## Goal
Test the improved workout-assistant setup on the same 5 cases and compare it to the baseline.

## Test Case 1
**User input:** Beginner user, chest day, no equipment, 20 minutes.

**Improved output:**
Target muscle group:
Arms

Recommended next step:
Do a 20-minute beginner arm session with no equipment: 3 sets of chair or wall tricep dips for 6–8 reps, 2 sets of slow push-ups from knees or against a wall for 8–10 reps, and 2 rounds of 20-second isometric bicep squeezes by pressing your hands against each other.

Reason:
This matches arm day, beginner level, no-equipment limits, and a short 20-minute session. It keeps the workout simple and manageable while still training the arms.

Safety adjustment:
Use wall push-ups instead of floor push-ups if needed, and keep the range of motion small on dips if your shoulders or elbows feel uncomfortable. Rest 45–60 seconds between sets.

Alternative easier option:
Do wall push-ups only, plus isometric arm squeezes and arm circles, for a lower-intensity version.
